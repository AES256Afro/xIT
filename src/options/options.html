xIT

Copied X links go through the redirector you pick. Nothing leaves your browser.

Default redirector

Used by the tweet button, the keyboard shortcut, and X's own “Copy link”.

Copy behaviour

Redirect page loads

Off by default. When on, visiting x.com sends you straight to your chosen frontend. Your logged-in X session stays untouched. You just stop landing on it.

Which pages

Your own home timeline, messages, notifications and settings are never redirected, since you would just be locked out of them.

Redirectors

Untick any you do not want cluttering the dropdown.

Custom redirector

For a self-hosted instance or anything not listed. Tokens: {path} {query} {user} {id} {hash} {host}. A plain mirror is usually https://your.host/{path}{query}.

Keyboard shortcut

Copy the tweet under the pointer with the default redirector. Default Alt+Shift+C.

To change it: Chrome chrome://extensions/shortcuts · Firefox about:addons → gear → Manage Extension Shortcuts. Those pages cannot be opened from here, so paste the address in yourself.
